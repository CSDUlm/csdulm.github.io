# PI portraits and research sources

Verified on 10 October 2026. The members list adds Julie Delon, Irène Waldspurger, Nicolas Schreuder and Yann Ollivier at the site owner's request. Their four email addresses come directly from that request. All 12 portraits below are photographs of the named researcher, verified against official or personal research pages. No generated portraits, stock faces or drawings are used.

The local assets are WebP conversions of the original photographs, limited to a maximum of 600 pixels in either dimension without enlargement. The original framing and aspect ratio are preserved in the files; the gallery applies a CSS crop with a face-sensitive `object-position`. WebP conversion removes source metadata. The gallery links to each photograph's source, and displays named photographer credits where verified.

## Rights and provenance

Only the Irène Waldspurger portrait has an explicit open license identified in its source. The other photographs were obtained from official institutional pages or the researchers' self-published websites, as requested. Availability on a public website does not establish an open reuse license; these images are **not** represented as Creative Commons or covered by the repository's software license. Photo-specific permission and authorship that the sources do not state are recorded as unknown. The centre can replace any of them with PI-supplied photos without changing the gallery layout.

## Research descriptions

The short English and French descriptions are concise paraphrases, not quotations. They describe research interests rather than infer current job titles or affiliations from older biographies.

- **Giulio Biroli:** [LPENS personal research profile](https://www.lpens.ens.psl.eu/giulio-biroli/), explicitly covering statistical physics of disordered classical/quantum systems, machine learning and theoretical ecology.
- **Olivier Cappé:** [Personal ENS page](https://www.di.ens.fr/~cappe/), describing statistics, machine learning, signal processing, recent online learning and privacy work.
- **Julie Delon:** [Personal page](https://judelo.github.io/), listing optimal transport, image processing, inverse problems and machine learning, corroborated by the [2026 ENS interview](https://www.ens.psl.eu/actualites/je-suis-persuadee-qu-fait-de-la-meilleure-science-lorsqu-ne-vient-pas-tous-du-meme-moule).
- **Emmanuel Dupoux:** [Personal LSCP page](http://www.lscp.net/persons/dupoux/), describing language acquisition and cognitive development with behavioural, brain-imaging and machine-learning models.
- **Marylou Gabrié:** [Personal page](https://marylou-gabrie.github.io/) and [2025 ENS interview](https://www.ens.psl.eu/en/node/8733), describing the interface of machine learning and statistical mechanics, generative models and sampling.
- **Bruno Loureiro:** [Personal page](https://brloureiro.github.io/), describing mathematical theory for modern machine-learning models at the interface with statistical mechanics. The real photograph was also visually cross-checked against his named [MAPLE project portrait](https://brloureiro.github.io/projects/maple/); the homepage's linocut is not used.
- **Stéphane Mallat:** [Personal ENS page and its publications/CV](https://www.di.ens.fr/~mallat/mallat.html), describing data science, deep learning, wavelets and multiscale signal representations.
- **Kimia Nadjahi:** [Personal page](https://kimiandj.github.io/), listing computational optimal transport, generative models, learning theory and model compression.
- **Yann Ollivier:** [Personal research page](https://www.yann-ollivier.org/rech/), describing probability and information theory for machine learning, with research articles on reinforcement learning.
- **Gabriel Peyré:** [2025 ENS interview](https://www.ens.psl.eu/en/node/8439), describing optimal transport, mathematical machine learning, imaging and single-cell genomics, alongside his [personal page](https://www.gpeyre.com/).
- **Nicolas Schreuder:** [Personal page](https://nicolasschreuder.github.io/), describing fairness, robustness, privacy and nonparametric two-sample testing.
- **Irène Waldspurger:** [Personal page](https://www.ceremade.dauphine.fr/~waldspurger/), describing non-convex optimization, inverse problems, low-rank matrix recovery and phase retrieval.

## Verification

All 12 originals were opened and inspected before conversion. Unrelated namesakes were excluded. Drawings found on Bruno Loureiro's homepage and Nicolas Schreuder's current site, and illustrated GitHub avatars, were excluded. Only the 12 optimized `.webp` assets are kept in the repository; source originals are not duplicated there.
