Affiliations describe the institution around the seminar date where dated evidence is available. Exact announcements, original dated papers, official annual reports and dated researcher career histories take precedence over a current job. For forthcoming seminars, the verified current affiliation is used and may need reconfirmation before the event. A small number of historical affiliations are inferred from dated evidence shortly before/after the event or appointments spanning the event; the evidence column states that basis.

Joint talks keep the existing single institution field: use one institution where shared, or explicitly name the speakers when their institutions differ. Sources are attached to each enriched database record as “Speaker affiliation” entries. Sources are provenance only and do not restore the removed public announcement links.
